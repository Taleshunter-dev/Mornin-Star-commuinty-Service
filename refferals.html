We believe that community life is essential. Morning Star’s Community Access & Participation service helps you venture beyond your home with confidence and safety. Whether you’re visiting friends, attending appointments or exploring hobbies, we support you to stay connected to the people and places that matter. Caring Supports emphasises that community access helps maintain relationships and pursue interests; our approach is just as empowering.
